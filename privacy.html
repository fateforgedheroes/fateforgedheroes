Privacy policy

Fate Forged Heroes · last updated 20 October 2026

Who we are

Fate Forged Heroes is made by J3DUIN, the Netherlands. Contact: fateforgedheroes@gmail.com.

What we store

If you create an account we store:

  • your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google;
  • your game progress (heroes, gear, currencies and settings), plus a limited number of earlier copies of it as a backup;
  • if you play the arena: your arena rating, your defense team and a log of your arena fights (kept for 30 days).
  • your friends list, your mail (gifts and rewards), your guild membership and your guild boss fights;
  • messages you write in the World and Guild chat (kept for 14 days), reports you make about messages, and, if a moderator mutes you, the mute;
  • technical data needed to keep you signed in, such as login tokens and the time of your last sign-in.

If you play without an account, your progress is only stored in your own browser and never sent to us.

Play statistics

To know how many people play and how far they get, the game sends us a small anonymous play signal, with or without an account: a random code it makes for your browser (stored in your browser, not linked to your name or email address), whether you are signed in, your player level, how many battles you have won, how many campaign stages you have cleared and whether you created your hero. It is sent when you open the game and at most every 15 minutes while you play. We do not store your IP address with it, and we delete these signals after 120 days.

We also count visits to the website with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you.

Why

We use this data only to run the game: to sign you in, to save and load your progress on any device, and to run the arena, guilds, friends, mail and chat (including keeping the chat safe). The play statistics only tell us how many people play and where new players stop, so we can make the game better. We do not sell your data and do not show ads based on it.

What other players see

If you have an account, other players can see your player name, avatar, player level and progress on the leaderboards (campaign, Boss Hall, Tower, Siege, arena, guilds). Arena opponents also see your defense team and rating. Your friends and guild mates can open your profile: your level, guild, progress, your best heroes with their gear and your arena rating. Chat messages are seen by everyone in that chat, with your name, avatar, level and guild tag. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Where it is stored

Accounts and saves are stored with our database provider Supabase. The website is hosted by Vercel, which also counts the anonymous visits. These providers process data on our behalf.

How long

We keep your data as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the game (account button, then "Delete my account"). This removes your account, your save, its backups and your arena data immediately.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data by emailing fateforgedheroes@gmail.com. You may also file a complaint with your data protection authority (in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
