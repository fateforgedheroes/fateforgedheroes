# Fate Forged Heroes: notes for Claude Code

Turn-based fantasy RPG in the browser (inspired by RAID: Shadow Legends). Owner: Jelte. Talk to him in **Dutch**; everything in the game is **English**.

Live: https://www.fateforgedheroes.com (Vercel, auto-deploys from `main`). Accounts and cloud saves: Supabase.

## How it is built

- No framework, no dependencies. `node build.mjs` concatenates the sources into `dist/index.html` (one ~11 MB page).
- `build.mjs` looks for each source in `src/`, `src/assets/`, `assets/`, `public/` or the repo root (the repo was once uploaded flat, so files may sit in the root).
- Vercel env vars `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` are injected at build time as `window.FFH_CONFIG`. Without them the game runs offline (localStorage only).

## Navigation (app.js)

There is no tab bar (`#tabs` is hidden by CSS). **Phones** (CSS `(max-width: 760px), (max-height: 500px) and (orientation: landscape)`): a slim sticky header with a gold **Home** button on every screen but Home (`#homebtn`; `body[data-tab]` is set in `render`), energy, Sigils, profile and a ☰ menu (`#menubtn`, `menuHtml`/`setMenu`: Guide, Mail with badge, profile, sound, music, every shard and stone count); the backBar's "‹ Home" is hidden there. The battle is one full-height screen (field, skill panel, log filling what is left; in landscape the skill panel sits right of the field and the log is hidden), and in landscape the homebase map fits the height. On a phone held upright the homebase shows a gold pulsing "Swipe left and right" hint (`.swipe-hint`) until player level 10 (not after a prestige). Screens with one main action put it in a fixed bottom bar (`.m-act`: Campaign Team edit + Fight, Team add/remove + Equip best gear), and the Team screen compacts to single scrolling rows. The next campaign battle lists the enemies of every phase (`.cn-phases`, all screen sizes). The main menu is **Home** (`tab = 'home'`): the homebase map with clickable buildings (`HOME_ZONES`: building box, name plate rect (`HZP(cx, cy)`, centred under the building) and plate art key, in image pixels). Active: Campaign, Boss Halls, Summon Altar (Fate Altar), Heroes & Gear (the forge; a `name` plate covers the painted name), Social (the campfire, `tab = social`: Friends and Guild tabs), Guild Hall (the building with the armillary sphere; `go: guild` opens the Social screen on its Guild tab, locked badge until Chapter II), Town Hall (profile, with the Sound setting; on phones under 480 px the header drops the sound button and the Ascension Stones counter), Arena (`tab = arena`). The header **Guide** button (`tab = guide`, `guideHtml`: every term and abbreviation in groups with a search box; lists come from engine data) and the header **Mail** button (`tab = mail`, badge from `paintMail`) lists friend requests, gifts and weekly arena rewards; `socialLoad` fetches friends and mail on sign-in, on opening Social or Mail and every 2 minutes. Zones without `go` (the Market) show their plate dimmed with a "Coming soon" tag, so those buildings can become any future mode: give the zone a `go`. Every other screen starts with `backBar()` ("‹ Home"; Heroes and Team share a switch). The logo also leads home. **First steps:** after picking a starter the player lands on Home with only the Campaign lit (`firstSteps()`: no campaign battle yet; a shade with a spotlight and a "Start here" marker, `CAMP_AT`); every other building and the profile button answer with a toast until the first campaign battle (won or lost), which shows the "Your homebase is open" unlock (`S.seen.home`). Its button starts the **homebase tour** (`TOUR`, `tourCard`): one building at a time with a spotlight and a card (what it is for, open or what opens it, Next / Skip); `S.seen.tour` marks it done (older saves past stage 1 skip it in `fixup`). The last step leads to the campaign. **Fight spotlight** (`fightSpot()`): before the first battle and right after the tour (`spotFight`) the campaign's Fight button is the only thing to press (shade + "Tap Fight to start"; other clicks are blocked by `.spot-block`).

## Game systems (engine.js)

- **Essences:** Ember → Verdant → Storm → Frost → Radiant → Umbral → Ember; Aether neutral. Strong Hit ×1.2 (crit ok, debuff ×1.15, 2 break), Normal ×1.0 (1 break), Weak ×0.75 (no crit, debuff ×0.5, 0 break).
- **Turn meter:** units fill by Speed to 100. `Battle.predict(n)` shows turn order.
- **Damage:** ATK × mult → crit (1 + cdmg%) → hit type → × 100/(100+DEF) → modifiers. Debuff chance: base × hitmod × (1 + (acc − res)/100), clamped.
- **Gift-only heroes:** Dio (Legendary Frost support: heals, cleanses, shields) and Malvek (Legendary Ember warrior, Fire Paladin) are like J3DUIN (`dev: true`, not in `CHAMP_ORDER`) but anyone who gets them may use them in the arena; send them with a mail gift `{"hero": "dio"}` / `{"hero": "malvek"}`. **Developer hero:** `j3duin` (J3DUIN, the owner's avatar) is a normal Legendary in `CHAMPS` with `dev: true` but not in `CHAMP_ORDER`, so no summon, unlock, capture or bot gives him (`K.DEV_HEROES`; lists in app.js add him only when owned). He only arrives as a mail gift `{"hero": "j3duin"}`, and the arena server (`DEV_HERO_OWNERS` in logic.js) rejects him in any team but the owner's account.
- **Heroes:** every hero has 3 skills (Karnok, Morgrim and Vorlund got a third; `fixup` pads old saves' skill levels, `checkTeam` accepts a shorter `sk` from older snapshots). 54 in `CHAMP_ORDER` (12 Uncommon, 14 Rare, 17 Epic, 11 Legendary; the four orcs at the end), plus the gift-only heroes (see Developer hero). Heroes 26-50 (`kaelira` … `celesthyr`) are Fate Altar only and reuse existing passive ids (incl. boss passives); `harmony` (Liora) is theirs alone. Legendaries only from the Fate Altar. Stat mult by rarity `RAR_MULT` (Common 0.9, Uncommon 0.95, Rare 1, Epic 1.12, Legendary 1.4); level cap `RAR_CAP` (Common/Uncommon/Rare 50, Epic/Legendary 60) = min(rarity cap, stars × 10), so a built-up Uncommon is a real team member, a bit below a Rare. **Team roles** (app.js `teamRoles`): the Team screen checks Damage, Healing, Protection and Control and warns when there is no healer; a defeat without one says so. Measured (end-of-Easy rosters): from Chapter IV on a team with a healer beats four attackers by 10-40%. Captured enemies become weak heroes (`captured: true`).
- **Bosses:** 25 in `BOSSES`, 2–3 phases, passive, Break Meter (Affinity Break = 2-turn stun, +15% damage taken). Per-boss power `BOSS_PW` was calibrated by simulation.
- **Own hero (Fateborn):** new players, players who start over and older saves without one (at their next start) first **create their own hero** (app.js `createHtml`, `createHero`; replaces the old starter choice): six class cards, then male or female, one of six classes (`K.PC_CLASSES`: Tank, Warrior, Mage, Ranger, Rogue (role Assassin) and Healer, each with its own 3 skills, passive and pitch; a Support class was dropped before release, `fixup` turns a test save's Support hero into a Healer) and a name. The hero is always **Aether** (no Strong or Weak Hits) and each class × gender is a hero id `pc_<class>_<m|f>` (`K.PC_IDS`, `pc: true`, Epic, not in `CHAMP_ORDER`: no summon, unlock or bot gives it; the gender only changes the art: each has its own animated sheet). `S.hero = { id, name, g, cls }`; `syncHero` (on every save) sets its name, its stars from its level (`K.pcStars`: 4★ up to 40, 5★ up to 50, 6★ after; no Ascension Stones) and its skills (`K.pcSkills`: +1 every 12 levels). It gains XP like any hero; an older save's hero starts at the level of its best hero. **Always at your side:** `keepHero` puts it first in every team (`linkTeams`) and every Tower team; it cannot be removed, fed or sent on an expedition, and as a joker it may climb every Tower of Essence (`twFits`). The arena server checks it too (`checkTeam`: one per team, stars and skills as its level gives). `S.needStarter`, `K.STARTERS`, `STARTER_SUB` and `stageUnlock(st, starter)` stay for old saves; `S.starter` is now the hero's id for new players (default avatar). Measured (campaign sim, one run per class): every class PASSES in ~850-3,300 battles (median ~2,200; a Healer hero or an early Legendary summon makes it shorter). Chapter I builds the team: stage 1 is solo vs 1 enemy per phase (a fixed mixed-essence line-up `CHAPTERS[0].first` at strength `FIRST_PW` 0.7 on Easy, so every starter, even an Uncommon, wins it), stages 2-3 are 2 enemies per phase (`CHAPTERS[0].shapes`), and first clears of stages 1-3 unlock Draelyn, Bromir and Skavren, who join the team while it has fewer than 4. Grythor, Vaessa and Brukkar unlock on stage 3 of Chapters II-IV.
- **Enemies:** 54 in `ENEMIES` (Common/Uncommon/Rare), all capturable via `CAPTURE_ROLE`. Enemies 26-50 have English ids (`gravestalker` … `thundergolem`); 51-54 are the orcs Grimtar (Verdant mage) and Zulgroth (Umbral support) in Chapter II, Bloodsnarl (Ember ranger with a wolf) and Krogash (Ember mage) in Chapter IV (art cut from painted cards; now redrawn in manga style facing right). The orcs are also full Uncommon heroes in `CHAMPS` with the same id (3 skills, starter choice and Fate Altar), so they are not in `CAPTURE_ROLE`: capturing one gives that hero. Each chapter has a themed `pool` (regular + elite, 8-11 distinct foes per chapter); `stageGroup` mixes them so the phases of a stage differ.
- **Campaign:** `CHAPTERS` (10 × 7 stages, names/desc/set per chapter). Stage 1–6 drop weapon/helmet/shield/gloves/chest/boots, stage 7 = chapter boss, random slot. Each chapter drops its own gear set.
- **Campaign difficulties** (`K.DIFFS`): Easy → Normal → Hard → Brutal → Nightmare, each the whole campaign again in a higher level band: enemy level = `base + stage level × slope` (`K.diffLvl(st, d)`: Normal ~19-64 … Nightmare ~51-84), strength `stageDiff(st, d)` = `f × (1 + chDiff × chapter × spread)`. So a harder difficulty is a challenge from its first chapter, not trivial early and a wall late. A difficulty opens once all 70 stages of the previous one are cleared. Gear drops only the difficulty's `rars` (Easy Uncommon/Rare, Normal Rare/Epic, Hard Epic/Legendary, Brutal Legendary, Nightmare Legendary/Mythical); `stageLoot(st, d)` gives the chance of the higher rarity (grows per chapter and on boss stages). Hero unlocks only happen on Easy. Save: `S.cleared` = Easy progress, `S.dcl[d]` = progress on difficulty d ≥ 1, `S.diff` = selected (the old Brutal mode `S.clearedHard` became Normal progress in `fixup()`).
- **Gear:** rarities 0-5; upgrades go to +16 (`MAX_GEAR_LVL`, a substat added or boosted at +4/8/12/16; main stat +4.5% per level, so +16 is what +12 used to be), the chance drops 6% per level down to 15% (`upgradeChance`), and substats roll in wide ranges around the old averages (`SUB_POOL`), so a great piece is a lucky roll worth farming for (RAID-style grind); index 5 **Mythical** (red, `--r5`) is gear only (Nightmare), 25% stronger substats (`subScale`). Loot tables: `genGear({ rars, up })`; without `rars` (starting gear) rarity rolls freely. Boss Hall loot: `bossLoot(n)` (Rare/Epic on levels 1-4, Epic/Legendary on 5-8, Legendary on 9-10). **Sets do not stack**: `activeSets` counts each set once. Crit rate is capped at `CRIT_CAP` (75%), also with buffs.
- **Phases:** every stage and Boss Hall level is `PHASES` (3) fights in a row. `STAGES[i].phases` holds 3 equal-size groups (`foes` = the last one; the chapter boss is in phase 3); `bossPhases(id, n)` = two groups of 3 minions sharing the boss's essence, then the boss. Between phases `phaseRest(heroes)`: survivors +15% HP, cooldowns reset, effects cleared, the fallen stay down. Rewards (`winXp`, `winSilver`, player XP) are per cleared stage and were doubled for this. The app walks survivors off to the right between phases (`nextPhase` in app.js).
- **Boss Hall:** 25 bosses × 10 levels. Every level plays like a campaign stage (`bossRoom(i, n)`): boss i belongs to chapter floor(i × 10 / 25); every two levels are one campaign difficulty (`bossDiff(n)`: 1-2 Easy, 3-4 Normal, 5-6 Hard, 7-8 Brutal, 9-10 Nightmare) with that difficulty's loot (`bossLoot`: Epic only from level 3, Legendary from level 5), the first level of a pair like the chapter's third stage, the second like its boss stage; enemies get that stage's level (`bossLvl`) and strength × `BOSS_ROOM` (1.1) via `bossUnits(id, n, p)`. So the Boss Hall is never a shortcut to better gear. The screen shows each level's difficulty, enemy level and drops. **Enrage** (Boss Hall only, `u.hall`; campaign bosses are left as they were): a Boss Hall boss counts its own turns; after `K.ENRAGE.at` (14; `blightAt` 22 for bosses with a Blight Aura, whose fights are meant to be long) it is enraged (+25% Attack) and gets another stack every 2 turns (`Battle.rage`, effect `enrage` with v = stacks), so a team that cannot kill it in time is worn down. The battle shows "⏳n" (turns left) and then "RAGE×n" on the boss, plus an Enraged banner (`enrage` hook). **Blight Aura** (`K.BLIGHT`, Boss Hall only): bosses from level 3 on have 2.2× HP and 0.5× Attack, and each of their turns (unless stunned, frozen or broken) costs every hero 11% of max HP on levels 3-4 (Normal) up to 14% on 9-10 (Nightmare), ignoring Defense (`Battle.aura`, effect `blight`). This makes a healer necessary: measured, a team without healing needs ~1.6× the power at those bosses, while with a healer (even the free Draelyn) the level is about as hard as without the aura. A level with the aura takes ~3 min at 1× (boss fight ~1.5 min; the boss acts ~12 times, max ~16, so enrage at 22 is only a backstop).
- **Energy** (`K.ENERGY`, like RAID): every campaign stage (Easy 3, Normal 4, Hard 6, Brutal 8, Nightmare 10; Easy Chapter I free the first time, a replay costs 3, `stageEnergy(st, d, first)`, app.js `stEn`) and Boss Hall level (6 + level, `bossEnergy`) costs energy when it starts, won or lost. +1 every 3 minutes up to `energyMax` (100 + player level); a player level-up adds energy on top (app.js `levelEnergy`: a full bar up to level `LEVEL_FULL_EN` 15, then `LEVEL_EN` 60; it may go over the max). Measured supply: early on level-ups alone give ~4× what battles cost (new players never wait); from Hard on regen (480 a day) + quests (~90 a day) + level-ups cover roughly 60 battles a day, so late players wait or buy refills with Gems. Arena and guild boss use their own tokens and keys. app.js: `S.energy` + `S.enAt` (ms of the last refill step; both filled lazily by `energyTick`), `spendEnergy` in `startCampaign` / `startDungeon`, the `#energy` counter in the header (under 480 px without the "/max" part and without the Fate Shard counter), costs on Fight / Replay / Challenge (`enCost`); Auto ×10 stops when the next battle cannot be paid. Pace: Easy ~2,000 battles ≈ 1-2 weeks, all difficulties a few months.
- **Boss Hall traits** (`K.BTRAIT`, from level 5 = Hard): like a RAID dungeon each boss needs a specific tool. Even bosses in `BOSS_ORDER` have **Iron Hide** (takes 60% less damage), odd ones **Swift** (+40% Speed); the first Defense Down / Speed Down that lands breaks the trait for the rest of the fight (`addEffect`). Shown as a badge on the boss, in the Boss Hall screen (`.bh-trait`, says whether your team can answer it) and as a defeat tip. Measured (Hard level 5): without the tool the win rate drops from ~50% to ~0-10%, with it nothing changes. So no team is forced onto one or two heroes, both tools are spread over every essence and rarity: Defense Down on Vaessa, Grimtar, Karnok, Vorlund, Brukkar (Uncommon), Nyressa, Korran, Elyndra (Rare), Zephara, Drakulen, Nyxara (Epic), Morveth, Draevan, Volkaris (Legendary) and the Warrior, Mage and Ranger own hero; Speed Down on 16 heroes, at least one per essence (Krogash and Morgrim for Ember, Kaelira and Seraphine for Radiant).
- **Expeditions** (the ship on the homebase, `tab = expedition`, opens after Chapter I; `K.EXPEDITIONS`, `expReward`): one voyage at a time with up to 4 heroes (picking one that is in a team asks to take it out of that team, unless it is the only hero there; `S.exp = { k, ids, start, end, lvl }`; heroes away cannot join a team or be fed, `onExp`; the crew shares its XP, so a hero at its level cap passes its share on). Coastal Patrol 1 h, The Sunken Ruins 12 h, The Far Isles 24 h. XP per hero = `xp × winXp(lvl)` and Sigils = `silver × winSilver(lvl)` with lvl the player's campaign level (`expLvl`); per hour 3 / 2 / 1.5 stages' worth, far below fighting. Shards and stones per trip (`fs` / `st`, below 1 = a chance). Rewards roll on collect (`expClaim`); Recall ends a trip without rewards. A dot on the ship when it is back.
- **Tower of Essence** (the Challenges building, `tab = tower`, opens after Chapter II; engine `K.TOWER`, `towerFloor`, `towerFoes`, `towerUnits`, `towerReward`): seven towers (`K.TOWERS`): six essence towers climbed with heroes of that essence only, and the Tower of Fate (`All`) for every hero, 300 floors each (`S.tw = { prog, team, cur }`; the team per tower, up to 4, not heroes away on an expedition). One fight per floor (`runBattle` with `nPh: 1`, `cfg.ids`), each floor pays once on its first clear (Sigils, XP; boss floors every 10th: Fate Shard + 2 Lesser; every 30th Greater shard + Greater stone; every 50th Ancient shard; every 100th Ancient stone; floor 300 a Mythic shard). No energy; a defeat pays nothing (tries are free). Every tower foe enrages like a Boss Hall boss (`u.hall`), so a fight neither side can win (e.g. a lone healer against healing foes) ends in a defeat instead of hitting the 300-turn cap. A team of fewer than four meets tougher foes (`TOWER.solo` by team size: ×3, ×1.7, ×1.25, ×1; the Tower screen warns): measured, a solo Legendary reaches ~40 after Easy and ~75 after Brutal, a full team ~140 and ~270. Enemies are fixed per tower and floor (hashed pick), with more counter-essence foes higher up; floor strength follows `TOWER.curve` ([floor, enemy level, strength] points). Calibrated (best Ember team, floor won half the time): Chapter III-IV ~60, Easy done ~130, Brutal start ~200, Brutal done ~270, Nightmare done ~300 (floor 300 ~20% a try); teams without a Legendary stop 30-70 floors earlier. The Tower of Fate has its foes ×2 (`TOWER.open`, boss floors ×1.15 `openBoss`): measured with four Legendaries it ends where the essence towers do (Brutal done ~260, floor 300 ~25% a try for a Nightmare-done team), four Epics stop ~90 floors earlier.
- **Fate Altar:** `FATE_SHARDS` (fate, greater, ancient, mythic, legendary) with drop chance per win and rarity table. A Fate Shard costs `SHARD_PRICE` (2500) Sigils. Pity: after `PITY_EPIC` (40) summons without an Epic or better, the next one is Epic (`S.pity`); only `PITY_SHARDS` (Ancient, Mythic, Legendary) count and trigger it.
- **Difficulty / grind:** `K.TUNE` in engine.js: `chDiff` (0.15 per chapter from Chapter III on), `chBoss` (0.7, the chapter boss unit itself), `bossWall` (1, whole boss stage), `wall` (1.4, see walls), `xp`/`silver` scale rewards. **Attrition** (`TUNE.hit` 0.75, `TUNE.foeHp` 1.5 from `TUNE.hitFrom` = Chapter III, regular campaign enemies only, chapter bosses untouched): softer hits but more HP, so fights are a steady stream of damage that healing absorbs; a team without healing needs ~1.4× the power (harder hits did the opposite: burst kills heroes before a healer helps). A middle way on purpose: in the campaign a healer clearly helps, in the Boss Hall (Blight Aura) it is required. **Walls:** two stages per chapter from Chapter II on (`K.WALLS`, never the boss stage; `isWall(st)`) are `TUNE.wall` stronger, so the player has to farm earlier stages first. They are deliberately not marked: players find out by losing and have to build their team up. `STAGE_PW` (engine.js) is a per-stage correction measured with `balance-sim.html?calib=1`: it removed spikes (4-foe elite stages, chapter bosses) so difficulty climbs ~5% per stage; re-measure it when enemies, chapter pools or stage shapes change. Walls are intended: late stages need farmed gear (upgrades cost Sigils) and summoned Epic/Legendary heroes. Measured: Easy ~1,500-4,300 battles (median ~2,600) with walls, enrage, the +16 gear grind and the stone tiers (`campaign-sim.cjs` must PASS 6×; its player brings a healer from Chapter IV on); all five difficulties ~5,000-16,000 battles for most players, unlucky rosters stay stuck on Nightmare until they summon better heroes (`balance-sim.html`, run it from a local web server next to engine.js).
- **Player profile** (`S.p`, app.js): name (first change free, then `RENAME_COST` = 2500 silver; `S.p.renames` counts changes), avatar (a hero id; without one the starter hero `S.starter` is shown, see `avatarId`), player level + XP, stats. The level stops at `PLAYER_MAX` (100); there the profile offers **Prestige** (`S.p.prestige`): back to level 1 with everything kept, `PRESTIGE_REWARD` (25,000 Sigils + a Mythic Fate Shard) and a new emblem (`presEmblem`: `PRESTIGE_ART[n - 1]` once that art exists, else a numbered gold badge; shown in the header and the profile). Unlocks and max energy use `effLvl` (100 for a prestiged player), so a prestige takes nothing away. Player XP comes from battles (`playerWinXp`, curve `pxNeed`); level-ups pay silver, every 5th level a Greater Fate Shard. `pxNeed(l) = 60l + 12l²`: level 10 (Boss Hall) takes ~60-75 battles, around Easy Chapter IV. `UNLOCKS` gates buildings: Fate Altar at player level 5 (`PLAYER_UNLOCK`, also in level-up messages), Arena and Guilds after clearing Chapter II and Boss Hall after Chapter III on Easy (`isOpen(s, t)`, `needTxt`/`needTag` for the texts and badges). The header `#account` button opens the profile screen (tab `profiel`), which also holds the cloud account section (`FFH_CLOUD.info()` / `openAccount()`).
- **Campaign screen:** **Phones** (`PHONE_MQ`, max 760 px) get a one-screen layout (`campPhoneHtml`): difficulty dropdown (`#cp-diff`) and chapter arrows on one row, the chapter name, a large set panel (`.cp-set`: icon, "This chapter drops", set name, piece count + bonus, pieces owned, rarities), a path of seven stage nodes (`csel` selects `campSel`), the selected stage's card (enemies per phase as portraits, drop, first-clear reward, enemy essences, Auto ×10 when cleared) and the team strip; Fight or Replay sits in the bottom bar. Desktop keeps the full layout. under the chapter title a set banner (`setBanner`) shows the set the chapter drops, its bonus, the rarities on this difficulty and how many pieces the player owns. A cleared stage tile has two buttons: Replay and **Auto ×10** (open once the whole chapter is cleared on that difficulty): `startCampaign(i, rep)` with `rep = { n, k, won }` plays the stage on auto, and after each win `repStep` starts the next one after a 3 s countdown (Stop keeps the result open; it waits while an unlock message is open); a defeat or the tenth battle ends the run. When a run ends (ten battles, a defeat, Stop or no energy) the result shows its totals (`repSummary`: Sigils, XP, player XP, shards, stones, captures, energy spent, levels reached, carried in `rep.sum`) and every piece of gear it dropped, with its stats on tap and "Equip on <hero>" for the hero it raises most (`bestWearer`: biggest power gain as a share of that hero's power, heroes in a team first; `repequip`). The Boss Hall has the same **Auto ×10** next to Challenge for a level already beaten (`startDungeon(id, n, rep)`); each battle pays its energy and the run stops when the next one cannot be paid.
- **Music** (`MUSIC` in app.js): background music made live with WebAudio from small synthesised instruments (`play`: strings, cello, staccato strings, violin, fiddle, horns and horn stabs, a formant choir, flute, lute, harp, upright bass, timpani, taiko, frame drum, tambourine, cymbal swell) through a convolution hall reverb and a compressor. `TRACKS`: `home` (a relaxed tavern tune in 6/8 for the homebase and every menu), `battle` (epic orchestral, D minor) and `boss` (darker, C minor; campaign boss stages, the Boss Hall and the guild boss; picked in `runBattle`). A track is chords per bar plus a melody in notes per eighth note; its `acc()` adds the accompaniment. A Web Worker tick schedules ahead, so it keeps time in a hidden tab. `S.music` (default on, button in the Town Hall settings) is separate from `S.sound`; it starts after the first click (browser rule). The header sound button mutes both and later turns back on what was on (`toggleAudio`, `S.audioWas`); the Town Hall and the ☰ menu switch each on its own and have a volume slider (`S.vol` 0-1, default 0.7, `VOL()`, scaling the effects' master gain and the music's `MUS_GAIN`).
- **Status badges** (app.js `fxBadge(k, n)`): buffs and debuffs on units are square icon badges (`FXP` paths via `FX_ICON`/`fxSvg`): green for buffs, red for debuffs, their own colours for Burn/Poison/Bleed/Freeze/Stun/Shield, ▲/▼ for stat ups and downs, turns left in a corner tag. Enrage pulses; the boss enrage clock is `.fxi.clock` (red at 3 turns or less). The Guide's effect list uses the same badges. Each badge's title says what it does and how many turns are left; phones have no hover, so a tap shows that text in a bubble (`#fx-tip`, caught before the tap reaches the unit underneath).
- **Auto battle** (`S.auto`) opens after the first clear of Chapter I · Stage 1 (`autoOk()`, with an unlock popup) and is remembered: once switched on it stays on for every battle until turned off. **Focus:** on auto (not in the arena) tapping an enemy sets `Battle.focus`; heroes aim single-target skills at it (`focusTarget`, taunt still wins), shown by a red target marker. **First battle coach:** in Chapter I · Stage 1 the first time (`TUT`), `coach()` explains goal, phases and turn order (card over the battlefield), gives tips on the first player turns and after phase 1, and the victory modal lists what to do next. The **Guide** button (`#b-help`) opens the guide in a popup (`#guide-pop`) and pauses the battle: `setPaused` stops the battle clock (`clock()`), and every battle wait (`later`, `sleep`, `tween`, `addFx`, projectiles) holds until it resumes. **Background play:** when the tab is hidden those waits run on a Web Worker timer (`bgSleep`, not throttled like page timers) and tweens step on it too (`kick()` on `visibilitychange`), so a battle plays on at its normal pace instead of freezing; only the drawing stops. **Damage meter:** `Battle.meter` (per unit: damage and healing by source, skill or effect name, so Burn/Poison/Bleed ticks count for whoever applied them; damage taken), added up over the phases (`addMeter`) and shown collapsed in the result modal (`meterHtml`).
- **Battle speed** (app.js `SPEED_UNLOCK`): 2× from Chapter I · Stage 4, 3× from Chapter III · Stage 1, 5× from Chapter II · Stage 1 but only when replaying a cleared stage or beaten Boss Hall level. `S.speed` is the preferred speed; `spd` is what the current battle runs at. At 5× (`calm()`) there are no screen flashes or shakes, hit flashes are faint and combat sounds are throttled (`SFX.calm`).
- **Feeding and skills:** any hero outside the team, or a spare copy (`S.fodder[id]`: captured enemies and Fate Altar duplicates), can be fed for XP (`feedXp`: a fixed amount by the food's rarity (`FEED_BASE`) and level, +10% per food level; it does not depend on the hero that eats it, so feeding matters less as heroes level up; Rare+ asks "Are you sure?"). A spare copy of the same hero raises its lowest skill by one level instead (`skillUp`). Skills max at `SKILL_MAX` (5), +`SKILL_STEP` (8%) power per level, cooldown −1 at max. The power multiplies the skill's damage, healing and shields (90% Attack becomes 97% at level 1); `skillLvTxt` shows it that way with the skill's own number.
- **Ascension Stones** buy stars, in three tiers by the star a hero ascends to (`K.STONES`, `stoneTier`, `rankCost` gives `tier`): Lesser to 2-4★ (`S.stones`, the old field), Greater to 5★ and Ancient to 6★ (8 each) in `S.stx`. Actions call `track(k, n)` (campaign win, energy spent, gear upgrade, feed/skill, summon, expedition, tower fight, Boss Hall win, arena attack, guild boss; lifetime counters in `S.q.tot`). **Missions** (`MISSIONS`): 5 chapters of 6 goals read from the save, claimed in order of chapters; chapter rewards include a random Epic (chapters 2 and 4) and Legendary hero (chapter 5) the player does not own yet (`rollHero`). **Collection** (`colList`): heroes and captured enemy kinds ever owned (`S.q.hs`, `S.q.be`, fed heroes still count), milestones per count, essence and rarity, plus the Bestiary. **Achievements** (`ACH`, tier rewards `ACH_RW`). No stat bonuses on purpose (the arena replays snapshots on the server). The Town Hall gets a dot on the homebase when something can be claimed (`thClaimN`).
- **Crystals and the Market** (app.js `S.gems`, `MARKET`, `marketHtml`, tab `market`, the tree building on the homebase): Crystals are the premium currency, called Crystals in the game but stored as `S.gems` (and `LEVEL_GEMS`, `GEM_SVG`, the mail gift key `gems`; keep those names) (header counter `#gems`, hidden under 480 px, and in the ☰ menu). Earned: `LEVEL_GEMS` (10) per player level, the daily 100-point chest (10), weekly chests (15/25/40), login day 7 (30), mission chapters (50-200), achievements (`ACH_RW`, 10-75), a complete essence in the collection (30) and mail gifts `{"gems": n}`. ## Save format (app.js)

`S` in localStorage key `ffh-save` (old key `kronen-van-as-v1` still read). On start the stored save (before migration) is copied into `ffh-save-backup` (every 12 h, newest 3); the profile screen lists them with a Restore button (`backupsHtml`, `restoreBackup`). Players can also **start over** themselves from the profile at any time (`startOverHtml` / `startOver`): the save becomes `resetSave()` (name kept) after a backup of the current one. Current version `v: 7`; `migrate()` upgrades older saves. When changing the save shape: bump `v`, add a migration step, keep old saves working. Small optional fields added within a version get a default in `fixup()`. Progress reset for everyone: `RESET` in app.js; any save (local or cloud) with a lower `reset` is replaced by `resetSave()` on load (keeps name and settings). Raising `RESET` wipes every player's progress once they open the game, so only do it on purpose. `save()` also queues a cloud upload.

Cloud sync (cloud.js): one row per account in `saves`. Each device stores in localStorage `ffh-cloud-base` which cloud version (`updated_at` string as returned by the server) its save is based on, plus a hash of the save at that moment. Uploads are a `PATCH … &updated_at=eq.<base>`; 0 rows updated means another device saved in between, so `sync()` runs: local unchanged → take the cloud silently; cloud unchanged → upload; both changed → the player picks a save in a dialog. Never upload without a base, and never decide by `savedAt` (it changes on every boot).

**Several accounts on one device** (cloud.js): the account menu has "Switch account" (the other accounts signed in here, with name and level) and "+ Add another account" (at most 5). `ffh-accounts` keeps each account's newest session (refreshed tokens are written back in `keep`), so switching needs no password. The account being played uses the normal keys (`SAVE_KEYS`: `ffh-save`, the old save key, `ffh-save-backup`, `ffh-cloud-base`); the others' copies wait under `<key>@<user id>`. A switch uploads the save, parks it (`stash`), swaps the other in (`unstash`) and reloads the page, so no state of the other account survives; not during a battle (`api.busy`). Adding parks the current account, clears the keys and shows the sign-in screen with "‹ Back to …" (`ffh-adding`). Signing out removes that account from the device and moves on to another one if there is one. When an account's sign-in has expired, the sign-in screen offers the other accounts; signing in as another account than the one whose save is on the device parks that save first (`expired`).

## Working rules

- After changing balance or engine: run `node campaign-sim.cjs` (or `npm test`) and keep it passing.
- Test in a browser (build, then open `dist/index.html`) before pushing; check the console for errors, also at 390 px width.
- Never put the Supabase service_role/secret key, Google client secret or database password in code.
- Anti-cheat: the campaign, loot and upgrades run client-side, so saves can be edited. In battle a manual choice is checked twice: the skill buttons check `usable()` themselves (not only the `disabled` attribute, which devtools can remove) and the engine (`Battle` turn loop) hands any choice that is not usable now (skill on cooldown, invalid target) to the AI; the arena server replays every move with the same check (`arenaReplay`). Arena results are decided on the server, but teams come from the save (checked for plausibility only). Anything that pays out between players needs a server-side check (Supabase Edge Function).
