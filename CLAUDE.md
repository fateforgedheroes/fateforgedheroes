# Fate Forged Heroes: notes for Claude Code

Turn-based fantasy RPG in the browser (inspired by RAID: Shadow Legends). Owner: Jelte. Talk to him in **Dutch**; everything in the game is **English**.

Live: https://www.fateforgedheroes.com (Vercel, auto-deploys from `main`). Accounts and cloud saves: Supabase.

## How it is built

- No framework, no dependencies. `node build.mjs` concatenates the sources into `dist/index.html` (one ~11 MB page).
- `build.mjs` looks for each source in `src/`, `src/assets/`, `assets/`, `public/` or the repo root (the repo was once uploaded flat, so files may sit in the root).
- **New version notice:** `build.mjs` stamps a build id (the first 12 hex of a SHA-256 of the page, or `FFH_BUILD` from the environment) into the page as `window.FFH_BUILD` and writes it to `dist/version.json` (`vercel.json` serves that file with `Cache-Control: no-store`). An open tab (app.js `checkVersion`) fetches it with `cache: 'no-store'` every 5 minutes while visible and whenever the tab is shown again; a different id shows the `#newver` popup over the whole game ("A new version is here", one button **Reload now**, `#newver-go`; `.newver` in shell.html): nothing else can be clicked and keys go only to that button, so nobody keeps playing on an old version. Never during a battle (or an Auto ×10 run): `paintVersion` hides it while `B` is set (called by `render()` and at the battle start), so it appears once the result is closed. The button (`reloadForVersion`) runs `save()`, uploads the save (`FFH_CLOUD.upload()`, at most 4 s) and reloads. Offline or from `file://` it stays silent.
- Vercel env vars `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` are injected at build time as `window.FFH_CONFIG`. Without them the game runs offline (localStorage only).

## Navigation (app.js)

There is no tab bar (`#tabs` is hidden by CSS). **Top bar** (`.wallet` in shell.html): every counter and button is an ornate plate (black face, double gold edge; "header plates" at the end of the style), Sound, Guide and Mail as icons only; Energy, Fate Shards and Stones have a gilded **+** (`.res-plus`) that opens the Market (the + after Sigils and Crystals is shelved until real-money sales: `.res-plus.shelved`, still in the markup) (not during a battle or the first steps; on phones under 480 px only on Energy). **During a battle** (also an Auto ×10 run; `body.in-battle`, set in `runBattle`, cleared in `endBattleView`) the header cannot leave the battle: the +, Mail, profile, logo and phone Home button are dimmed and a tap says "Not during a battle…"; Guide opens the in-battle guide popup, Sound and the ☰ menu (the volume sliders) keep working. **Side bar** (`sideNav`, `.snav`): every screen but Home and the battle has the Home buildings as a column of icons on the left (the round medallion at the left end of each `MENU_ART` banner, cropped by CSS), the open one lit, locked ones dimmed, the same dots as on Home; sticky. On phones it is left out: the header's **Menu** button opens the same list instead. Screens render inside `.navwrap` > `.navmain` (also the Town Hall's own redraw in `paintAccount`). **Phones** (CSS `(max-width: 760px), (max-height: 500px) and (orientation: landscape)`): a slim sticky header with a gold **Menu** button on every screen but Home (`#homebtn`; ## Game systems (engine.js)

- **Essences:** Ember → Verdant → Storm → Frost → Radiant → Umbral → Ember; Aether neutral. Strong Hit ×1.2 (crit ok, debuff ×1.15, 2 break), Normal ×1.0 (1 break), Weak ×0.75 (no crit, debuff ×0.5, 0 break).
- **Turn meter:** units fill by Speed to 100. `Battle.predict(n)` shows turn order.
- **Damage:** ATK × mult → crit (1 + cdmg%) → hit type → × 100/(100+DEF) → modifiers. Debuff chance: base × hitmod × (1 + (acc − res)/100), clamped.
- **Gift-only heroes:** Dio (Legendary Frost support: heals, cleanses, shields) and Malvek (Legendary Ember warrior, Fire Paladin) are like J3DUIN (`dev: true`, not in `CHAMP_ORDER`) but anyone who gets them may use them in the arena; send them with a mail gift `{"hero": "dio"}` / `{"hero": "malvek"}`. **Developer hero:** `j3duin` (J3DUIN, the owner's avatar) is a normal Legendary in `CHAMPS` with `dev: true` but not in `CHAMP_ORDER`, so no summon, unlock, capture or bot gives him (`K.DEV_HEROES`; lists in app.js add him only when owned). He only arrives as a mail gift `{"hero": "j3duin"}`, and the arena server (`DEV_HERO_OWNERS` in logic.js) rejects him in any team but the owner's account.
- **Heroes:** every hero has 3 skills (Karnok, Morgrim and Vorlund got a third; `fixup` pads old saves' skill levels, `checkTeam` accepts a shorter `sk` from older snapshots). 54 in `CHAMP_ORDER` (12 Uncommon, 14 Rare, 17 Epic, 11 Legendary; the four orcs at the end), plus the gift-only heroes (see Developer hero). Heroes 26-50 (`kaelira` … `celesthyr`) are Fate Altar only and reuse existing passive ids (incl. boss passives); `harmony` (Liora) is theirs alone. Legendaries only from the Fate Altar. Stat mult by rarity `RAR_MULT` (Common 0.9, Uncommon 0.95, Rare 1, Epic 1.12, Legendary 1.4); level cap `RAR_CAP` (Common/Uncommon/Rare 50, Epic/Legendary 60) = min(rarity cap, stars × 10), so a built-up Uncommon is a real team member, a bit below a Rare. **Team roles** (app.js `teamRoles`): the Team screen checks Damage, Healing, Protection and Control (`rolesHtml`: four small round icons only, a missing role dimmed with a dashed ring, the name and what it is for in the tooltip) and warns when there is no healer; a defeat without one says so. Measured (end-of-Easy rosters): from Chapter IV on a team with a healer beats four attackers by 10-40%. Captured enemies become weak heroes (`captured: true`).
- **Bosses:** 25 in `BOSSES`, 2–3 phases, passive, Break Meter (Affinity Break = 2-turn stun, +15% damage taken). Per-boss power `BOSS_PW` was calibrated by simulation.
- **Own hero (Fateborn):** new players, players who start over and older saves without one (at their next start) first **create their own hero** (app.js `createHtml`, `createHero`; replaces the old starter choice; the ornate look `.pcx` in shell.html: each class card filled with its painted art (`cards.js`, else the hero's portrait) in its class colour `PC_COL` with its icon `PC_IC` and name, gold numbered steps, Male ♂ / Female ♀, a dice `pcdice` for a random name from `PC_NAMES`, a gilded Create button with diamond ends, and a details panel lit in the class colour with the hero large on its right, stat icons `STAT_IC` and skill icons in the battle's glyphs; computers show the six cards in one row): six class cards, then male or female, one of six classes (`K.PC_CLASSES`: Tank, Warrior, Mage, Ranger, Rogue (role Assassin) and Healer, each with its own 3 skills, passive and pitch; a Support class was dropped before release, `fixup` turns a test save's Support hero into a Healer) and a name. The hero is always **Aether** (no Strong or Weak Hits) and each class × gender is a hero id `pc_<class>_<m|f>` (`K.PC_IDS`, `pc: true`, Epic, not in `CHAMP_ORDER`: no summon, unlock or bot gives it; the gender only changes the art: each has its own animated sheet). `S.hero = { id, name, g, cls }`; `syncHero` (on every save) sets its name, its stars from its level (`K.pcStars`: 4★ up to 40, 5★ up to 50, 6★ after; no Ascension Stones) and its skills (`K.pcSkills`: +1 every 12 levels). It gains XP like any hero; an older save's hero starts at the level of its best hero. **Always at your side:** `keepHero` puts it first in every team (`linkTeams`) and every Tower team; it cannot be removed, fed or sent on an expedition, and as a joker it may climb every Tower of Essence (`twFits`). The arena server checks it too (`checkTeam`: one per team, stars and skills as its level gives). **Fate Changing Stone** (app.js `S.fcs = { n, got, at }`, `FCS_PRICE`, `FCS_CD`, `fcsHtml`, `changeHero`): a Market offer (the first one free, then 50 Crystals; one held at a time) that turns the Fateborn into another class and/or gender (`fcsOpen`: the six classes, Male/Female, its skills and the stat change with the current gear; opened from the hero's details "Change class", the Market's Use button or the stone in Forge → Items). `changeHero` moves the roster entry (level, XP; stars and skills follow the level), all gear (`it.owner`, locks kept), its places in every team and Tower team, the avatar and `S.hero`; the name stays. One change every 72 hours (`fcsWait`). `S.needStarter`, `K.STARTERS`, `STARTER_SUB` and `stageUnlock(st, starter)` stay for old saves; `S.starter` is now the hero's id for new players (default avatar). Measured (campaign sim, one run per class): every class PASSES in ~850-3,300 battles (median ~2,200; a Healer hero or an early Legendary summon makes it shorter). Chapter I builds the team: stage 1 is solo vs 1 enemy per phase (a fixed mixed-essence line-up `CHAPTERS[0].first` at strength `FIRST_PW` 0.7 on Easy, so every starter, even an Uncommon, wins it), stages 2-3 are 2 enemies per phase (`CHAPTERS[0].shapes`), and first clears of stages 1-3 unlock Draelyn, Bromir and Skavren, who join the team while it has fewer than 4. Grythor, Vaessa and Brukkar unlock on stage 3 of Chapters II-IV.
- **Enemies:** 54 in `ENEMIES` (Common/Uncommon/Rare), all capturable via `CAPTURE_ROLE`. Enemies 26-50 have English ids (`gravestalker` … `thundergolem`); 51-54 are the orcs Grimtar (Verdant mage) and Zulgroth (Umbral support) in Chapter II, Bloodsnarl (Ember ranger with a wolf) and Krogash (Ember mage) in Chapter IV (art cut from painted cards; now redrawn in manga style facing right). The orcs are also full Uncommon heroes in `CHAMPS` with the same id (3 skills, starter choice and Fate Altar), so they are not in `CAPTURE_ROLE`: capturing one gives that hero. Each chapter has a themed `pool` (regular + elite, 8-11 distinct foes per chapter); `stageGroup` mixes them so the phases of a stage differ.
- **Campaign:** `CHAPTERS` (10 × 7 stages, names/desc/set per chapter). Stage 1–6 drop weapon/helmet/shield/gloves/chest/boots, stage 7 = chapter boss, random slot. Each chapter drops its own gear set.
- **Campaign difficulties** (`K.DIFFS`): Easy → Normal → Hard → Brutal → Nightmare, each the whole campaign again in a higher level band: enemy level = `base + stage level × slope` (`K.diffLvl(st, d)`: Normal 57-64, Hard 62-70, Brutal 68-77, Nightmare 75-84; Easy 1-61). **Every difficulty starts where the one before it ended:** its chapter peaks (the boss stages) climb evenly from the previous difficulty's last boss to its own last boss (those ends were kept), and each chapter is a sawtooth: stage 1 is 95% of the peak before it, stage 2 97.5%, stage 3 back at that peak, stages 4-7 climb to the new peak, so stage 7 is always the hardest. Measured strength (Easy I-4 = 1): Easy 1 → 33, Normal 31 → 45, Hard 43 → 68, Brutal 65 → 84, Nightmare 80 → 109. Easy keeps its own curve (`stageDiff` × `STAGE_PW`, `FIRST_PW`); Normal and up use `DIFF_PW[d - 1][stage]`, measured so each stage lands on the curve (enemy HP, Attack and Defense with the composition correction STAGE_PW gives Easy), play Chapter I with the usual groups instead of the tutorial ones (`hiPhases`, `stagePhases(st, d)`; the app shows and catches from those), use attrition in every chapter, and have soft walls (`wallMult`: 99% of the chapter's boss stage instead of `TUNE.wall`).
 A difficulty opens once all 70 stages of the previous one are cleared. Gear drops only the difficulty's `rars` (Easy Uncommon/Rare, Normal Rare/Epic, Hard Epic/Legendary, Brutal Legendary, Nightmare Legendary/Mythical); `stageLoot(st, d)`: the first rarity 80%, the second 20% (`LOOT_UP`), the same on every stage; the screens show both chances (`rarsHtml`). Hero unlocks only happen on Easy. Save: `S.cleared` = Easy progress, `S.dcl[d]` = progress on difficulty d ≥ 1, `S.diff` = selected (the old Brutal mode `S.clearedHard` became Normal progress in `fixup()`).
- **Gear:** rarities 0-5; upgrades go to +16 (`MAX_GEAR_LVL`, a substat added or boosted at +4/8/12/16; main stat +4.5% per level, so +16 is what +12 used to be), the chance per try falls with the level (`UPG_CHANCE` / `upgradeChance`: 100% at +0, 95, 90, 85, 80, 72, 65, 58, 50, 44, 38, 32, 26, 20, 16 and 12% for the last step to +16, so low levels hardly fail and the top is a gamble; every try gives no message: the pressed button glows gold on a success and red on a fail, `tryUpgrade` returns `ok` / `fail`, `upgGlow` lights the redrawn button, also for To +N (gold when it reached the milestone) and the Auto ×10 summary; `upgFx` stays for ascension), and substats roll in wide ranges around the old averages (`SUB_POOL`), so a great piece is a lucky roll worth farming for (RAID-style grind); index 5 **Mythical** (red, `--r5`) is gear only (Nightmare), 25% stronger substats (`subScale`). **Item level** (`it.il`, the level of the enemies it dropped from; it scales the main stat and substats) is hidden from players on purpose: no screen shows it, it only sorts gear behind the scenes, so an item shows rarity, +upgrade and ascension stars only. Loot tables: `genGear({ rars, up })`; without `rars` (starting gear) rarity rolls freely. Boss Hall loot: `bossLoot(n)`, the rarities of the level's campaign difficulty at the same 80/20 (Uncommon/Rare on levels 1-2 … Legendary/Mythical on 9-10). **Sets do not stack**: `activeSets` counts each set once. **Boss sets** (`BOSS_SET_LIST`, ids `bs_<boss id>`, `bossSets(i)`): every Boss Hall boss drops its own six-piece set with bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 pieces (`tiers`: a stat, a mechanic, a signature effect), each with a role (tank, poison, healer, assassin…) as a side-grade, not a ladder; the mechanics are flags read by the Battle (`setStart` in `start()`, `setTurn` at the start of a turn, `setHit` on every damaging hit, modifiers in `calc`, saves and low-HP triggers in `damage`, `heal`, `addEffect`, the Frozen Nova in `endTurn`, the Tidal Wave in `turn`, chain lightning in `perform`; new effect `iceTomb`). 4+ pieces from boss sets of one essence spread over 2+ different sets add a family bonus (`SET_FAMILY`; Radiant has one boss, so none). `setTiers(items)` lists every active bonus (campaign sets, boss tiers, families); `heroStats` applies them. The campaign sets stay 2- and 4-piece sets; the old Boss Hall sets (Fury, Nightshard, Vengeance, Ashcurse) no longer drop but keep working. Tested: 50 arena fights with every boss set, no errors, every server replay identical. **Item Ascension** (`MAX_ASC` 3, `ASC_COST`, `ascCost(it)`): any item at +16 can ascend three times, always succeeding, for Ascension Stones and Sigils (4 Greater + 25,000, 8 Greater + 50,000, 4 Ancient + 100,000; a deliberate Sigil sink); each step adds +10% to all its stats (`gearStats`) and a 50% higher sell value; shown as orange stars (app.js `ascStars`, `.asc-st`, on the icon via `gearIcon` and after the name via `itemNameH`), ascended from the gear popup (`ascHtml`, `ascBtn`, action `ascend`), the tile's Ascend button or the vault. Ascended gear is worn only by heroes of `ASC_STARS` (5) stars or more, whatever their rarity (`wearOk` on equip, Equip best gear, the Auto ×10 equip and `bestWearer`; `checkTeam` on the arena server rejects it otherwise, and ascension above 3 or below +16). With all six pieces of a boss set at ★★★ its 6-piece bonus becomes the stronger Ascended version (`ASC6`, a tier with `asc: true`; the battle reads the flag value 2). Selling an ascended piece asks first (stones are not returned); the vault filter "Ascended" lists them. Tested: 25 arena fights, every boss set fully ascended, server replays identical.
 Crit rate is capped at `CRIT_CAP` (75%), also with buffs.
- **Phases:** every stage and Boss Hall level is `PHASES` (3) fights in a row. `STAGES[i].phases` holds 3 equal-size groups (`foes` = the last one; the chapter boss is in phase 3); `bossPhases(id, n)` = two groups of 3 minions sharing the boss's essence, then the boss. Between phases `phaseRest(heroes)`: survivors +15% HP, cooldowns reset, effects cleared, the fallen stay down. Rewards (`winXp`, `winSilver`, player XP) are per cleared stage and were doubled for this. The app walks survivors off to the right between phases (`nextPhase` in app.js).
- **Boss Hall:** 25 bosses × 10 levels. Every level plays like a campaign stage (`bossRoom(i, n)`): boss i belongs to chapter floor(i × 10 / 25); every two levels are one campaign difficulty (`bossDiff(n)`: 1-2 Easy, 3-4 Normal, 5-6 Hard, 7-8 Brutal, 9-10 Nightmare) with that difficulty's loot (`bossLoot`: Epic only from level 3, Legendary from level 5), the first level of a pair like the chapter's third stage, the second like its boss stage; enemies get that stage's level (`bossLvl`) and strength via `bossUnits(id, n, p)` (Easy levels: `stageDiff` × `BOSS_ROOM` 1.1; levels 3-10: `ROOM_PW`, set so each level changed as much as its campaign stage when the difficulties were made to start where the one before ended). **Levels open with the campaign** (app.js `bhLvlOpen`, `diffOpen`): 3-4 once Normal is open (all of Easy cleared), 5-6 with Hard, 7-8 with Brutal, 9-10 with Nightmare; the level buttons show a lock and the screen says what opens them. So the Boss Hall is never a shortcut to better gear. **The screen** is laid out like the Campaign: first every boss in a list under each other (`bossListHtml`, `BV.view = 'bosses'`, chapter-style banners `.bl-ch`: portrait, boss number, essence and archetype, passive, progress over the ten levels, its set and what the next level drops; locked ones say who to beat first); tapping one (`bhopen`) opens its levels (`dungeonsHtml`, `BV.view = 'levels'`, "‹ Bosses" `bhlist`): on the left (`.bs-side`) its picture large over its chapter's background (`bossBig`), essence / archetype / phases / Break, passive, who is strong against it, its trait, its six-piece set with every bonus as a tight list (`.bs-info`, `.bs-tiers`; long lines cut short with the full text in their tooltip; the team is picked on the team screen Fight opens); on the right a row per level (`.sv-row`: drop icons, Level n and its difficulty, cleared / next / locked, enemy level, Auto ×10 once beaten, **Fight** with the energy cost); tapping a row (`bhlvl`, `BV.open`) shows its phases, the trait and the Blight Aura note. **Fight opens the team screen first** (`openBossPrep`, the same `prepHtml` as the Campaign with `PREP.boss`: the team for the boss's essence, picker `data-pp="bteam"`, enemy level, drops, energy, the phases, Auto ×10 and Fight `ppgo` → `startDungeon`); the result's Edit team returns there. On a phone on its side both columns scroll on their own (`.sv-wrap.lfit`, also the Campaign's stages) and the page never does. The screen shows each level's difficulty, enemy level and drops. **Enrage** (Boss Hall only, `u.hall`; campaign bosses are left as they were): a Boss Hall boss counts its own turns; after `K.ENRAGE.at` (14; `blightAt` 22 for bosses with a Blight Aura, whose fights are meant to be long) it is enraged (+25% Attack) and gets another stack every 2 turns (`Battle.rage`, effect `enrage` with v = stacks), so a team that cannot kill it in time is worn down. The battle shows "⏳n" (turns left) and then "RAGE×n" on the boss, plus an Enraged banner (`enrage` hook). **Blight Aura** (`K.BLIGHT`, Boss Hall only): bosses from level 3 on have 2.2× HP and 0.5× Attack, and each of their turns (unless stunned, frozen or broken) costs every hero 11% of max HP on levels 3-4 (Normal) up to 14% on 9-10 (Nightmare), ignoring Defense (`Battle.aura`, effect `blight`). This makes a healer necessary: measured, a team without healing needs ~1.6× the power at those bosses, while with a healer (even the free Draelyn) the level is about as hard as without the aura. A level with the aura takes ~3 min at 1× (boss fight ~1.5 min; the boss acts ~12 times, max ~16, so enrage at 22 is only a backstop).
- **Energy** (`K.ENERGY`, like RAID): every campaign stage (Easy 3, Normal 4, Hard 6, Brutal 8, Nightmare 10; Easy Chapter I free the first time, a replay costs 3, `stageEnergy(st, d, first)`, app.js `stEn`) and Boss Hall level (6 + level, `bossEnergy`) costs energy when it starts; **a defeat gives half of it back** (also when given up; `refund` in `finishBattle`, shown in the result and counted in the Auto ×10 totals). +1 every 3 minutes up to `energyMax` (**200** + player level, so a night's sleep fits); level-ups (app.js `levelEnergy`: a full bar up to level `LEVEL_FULL_EN` 15, then `LEVEL_EN` 60), quest chests, Market refills and gifts add energy on top of the bar through `addEnergy`, but never past `K.ENERGY.over` (2) × the bar (`enCeil`; the rest is lost, a Market refill that would go past it is refused, and `fixup` cuts older saves down to it). Measured supply: early on level-ups alone give ~4× what battles cost (new players never wait); from Hard on regen (480 a day) + quests (~90 a day) + level-ups cover roughly 60 battles a day, so late players wait or buy refills with Gems. - **Gear by role** (app.js `gearFit`, `roleW`, `ROLE_W`, `SET_FIT`, `fitGain`): Equip best, the ▲ and "Best first" in the gear bag and "Equip on …" after an Auto ×10 run choose gear for what the hero's role needs, not by raw Power (Power stays the number shown). ## Save format (app.js)

`S` in localStorage key `ffh-save` (old key `kronen-van-as-v1` still read). On start the stored save (before migration) is copied into `ffh-save-backup` (every 12 h, newest 3); the profile screen lists them with a Restore button (`backupsHtml`, `restoreBackup`). Players can also **start over** themselves from the profile at any time (`startOverHtml` / `startOver`): the save becomes `resetSave()` (name kept) after a backup of the current one. Current version `v: 7`; `migrate()` upgrades older saves. When changing the save shape: bump `v`, add a migration step, keep old saves working. Small optional fields added within a version get a default in `fixup()`. Progress reset for everyone: `RESET` in app.js; any save (local or cloud) with a lower `reset` is replaced by `resetSave()` on load (keeps name and settings). Raising `RESET` wipes every player's progress once they open the game, so only do it on purpose. `save()` also queues a cloud upload.

Cloud sync (cloud.js): one row per account in `saves`. Each device stores in localStorage `ffh-cloud-base` which cloud version (`updated_at` string as returned by the server) its save is based on, plus a hash of the save at that moment. Uploads are a `PATCH … &updated_at=eq.<base>`; 0 rows updated means another device saved in between, so `sync()` runs: local unchanged → take the cloud silently; cloud unchanged → upload; both changed → the player picks a save in a dialog. Never upload without a base, and never decide by `savedAt` (it changes on every boot). Saves are compared without the fields the game changes by itself or that are only settings (`VOLATILE`: savedAt, energy, enAt, disc, q, mk, sound, music, vol, mvol, cblock, cbname, audioWas, speed, auto, chap, diff, shake; `content`), so a device that was only opened again (energy refilled, a new day reset the quests) counts as unchanged and quietly takes the cloud instead of asking; the base keeps that hash as `h2` next to the old `hash`, which bases from older versions are still checked with (`unchanged`).

**Several accounts on one device** (cloud.js): the account menu has "Switch account" (the other accounts signed in here, with name and level) and "+ Add another account" (at most 5). `ffh-accounts` keeps each account's newest session (refreshed tokens are written back in `keep`), so switching needs no password. The account being played uses the normal keys (`SAVE_KEYS`: `ffh-save`, the old save key, `ffh-save-backup`, `ffh-cloud-base`); the others' copies wait under `<key>@<user id>`. A switch uploads the save, parks it (`stash`), swaps the other in (`unstash`) and reloads the page, so no state of the other account survives; not during a battle (`api.busy`). Adding parks the current account, clears the keys and shows the sign-in screen with "‹ Back to …" (`ffh-adding`). Signing out removes that account from the device and moves on to another one if there is one. When an account's sign-in has expired, the sign-in screen offers the other accounts; signing in as another account than the one whose save is on the device parks that save first (`expired`).

## Working rules

- After changing balance or engine: run `node campaign-sim.cjs` (or `npm test`) and keep it passing.
- Test in a browser (build, then open `dist/index.html`) before pushing; check the console for errors, also at 390 px width.
- Never put the Supabase service_role/secret key, Google client secret or database password in code.
- Anti-cheat: the campaign, loot and upgrades run client-side, so saves can be edited. In battle a manual choice is checked twice: the skill buttons check `usable()` themselves (not only the `disabled` attribute, which devtools can remove) and the engine (`Battle` turn loop) hands any choice that is not usable now (skill on cooldown, invalid target) to the AI; the arena server replays every move with the same check (`arenaReplay`). Arena results are decided on the server, but teams come from the save (checked for plausibility only). Anything that pays out between players needs a server-side check (Supabase Edge Function).
