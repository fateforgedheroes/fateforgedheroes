# Fate Forged Heroes: notes for Claude Code

Turn-based fantasy RPG in the browser (inspired by RAID: Shadow Legends). Owner: Jelte. Talk to him in **Dutch**; everything in the game is **English**.

Live: https://www.fateforgedheroes.com (Vercel, auto-deploys from `main`). Accounts and cloud saves: Supabase.

## How it is built

- No framework, no dependencies. `node build.mjs` concatenates the sources into `dist/index.html` (one ~8 MB page).
- `build.mjs` looks for each source in `src/`, `src/assets/`, `assets/`, `public/` or the repo root (the repo was once uploaded flat, so files may sit in the root).
- Vercel env vars `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` are injected at build time as `window.FFH_CONFIG`. Without them the game runs offline (localStorage only).

## Navigation (app.js)

There is no tab bar (`#tabs` is hidden by CSS). The main menu is **Home** (`tab = 'home'`): the homebase map with clickable buildings (`HOME_ZONES`: building box + name plate in image pixels). Active: Campaign, Boss Halls, Summon Altar (Fate Altar), Heroes & Gear (the forge; a `name` plate covers the painted name), Social (the campfire, `tab = social`: Friends and Guild tabs), Guild Hall (the building with the armillary sphere; `go: guild` opens the Social screen on its Guild tab, locked badge until Chapter II), Town Hall (profile, with the Sound setting; on phones under 480 px the header drops the sound button and the Ascension Stones counter), Arena (`tab = arena`). The header **Guide** button (`tab = guide`, `guideHtml`: every term and abbreviation in groups with a search box; lists come from engine data) and the header **Mail** button (`tab = mail`, badge from `paintMail`) lists friend requests, gifts and weekly arena rewards; `socialLoad` fetches friends and mail on sign-in, on opening Social or Mail and every 2 minutes. Zones without `go` show a "Coming soon" plate that covers the painted name, so those buildings (forge, arena, research, ship, market, challenge gate) can become any future mode: give the zone a `go`. Every other screen starts with `backBar()` ("‹ Home"; Heroes and Team share a switch). The logo also leads home. **First steps:** after picking a starter the player lands on Home with only the Campaign lit (`firstSteps()`: no campaign battle yet; a shade with a spotlight and a "Start here" marker, `CAMP_AT`); every other building and the profile button answer with a toast until the first campaign battle (won or lost), which shows the "Your homebase is open" unlock (`S.seen.home`). Its button starts the **homebase tour** (`TOUR`, `tourCard`): one building at a time with a spotlight and a card (what it is for, open or what opens it, Next / Skip); `S.seen.tour` marks it done (older saves past stage 1 skip it in `fixup`). The last step leads to the campaign. **Fight spotlight** (`fightSpot()`): before the first battle and right after the tour (`spotFight`) the campaign's Fight button is the only thing to press (shade + "Tap Fight to start"; other clicks are blocked by `.spot-block`).

## Game systems (engine.js)

- **Essences:** Ember → Verdant → Storm → Frost → Radiant → Umbral → Ember; Aether neutral. Strong Hit ×1.2 (crit ok, debuff ×1.15, 2 break), Normal ×1.0 (1 break), Weak ×0.75 (no crit, debuff ×0.5, 0 break).
- **Turn meter:** units fill by Speed to 100. `Battle.predict(n)` shows turn order.
- **Damage:** ATK × mult → crit (1 + cdmg%) → hit type → × 100/(100+DEF) → modifiers. Debuff chance: base × hitmod × (1 + (acc − res)/100), clamped.
- **Gift-only heroes:** Dio (Legendary Frost support: heals, cleanses, shields) and Malvek (Legendary Ember warrior, Fire Paladin) are like J3DUIN (`dev: true`, not in `CHAMP_ORDER`; art appended to heroes.js and portraits.js) but anyone who gets them may use them in the arena; send them with a mail gift `{"hero": "dio"}` / `{"hero": "malvek"}`. **Developer hero:** `j3duin` (J3DUIN, the owner's avatar) is a normal Legendary in `CHAMPS` with `dev: true` but not in `CHAMP_ORDER`, so no summon, unlock, capture or bot gives him (`K.DEV_HEROES`; lists in app.js add him only when owned). He only arrives as a mail gift `{"hero": "j3duin"}`, and the arena server (`DEV_HERO_OWNERS` in logic.js) rejects him in any team but the owner's account.
- **Heroes:** 50 in `CHAMP_ORDER` (8 Uncommon, 14 Rare, 17 Epic, 11 Legendary), plus the gift-only heroes (see Developer hero). Heroes 26-50 (`kaelira` … `celesthyr`) are Fate Altar only and reuse existing passive ids (incl. boss passives); `harmony` (Liora) is theirs alone. Legendaries only from the Fate Altar. Stat mult by rarity `RAR_MULT` (Common 0.9, Uncommon 0.95, Rare 1, Epic 1.12, Legendary 1.4); level cap `RAR_CAP` (Common/Uncommon/Rare 50, Epic/Legendary 60) = min(rarity cap, stars × 10), so a built-up Uncommon is a real team member, a bit below a Rare. **Team roles** (app.js `teamRoles`): the Team screen checks Damage, Healing, Protection and Control and warns when there is no healer; a defeat without one says so. Measured (end-of-Easy rosters): from Chapter IV on a team with a healer beats four attackers by 10-40%. Captured enemies become weak heroes (`captured: true`).
- **Bosses:** 25 in `BOSSES`, 2–3 phases, passive, Break Meter (Affinity Break = 2-turn stun, +15% damage taken). Per-boss power `BOSS_PW` was calibrated by simulation.
- **New players:** start with no heroes (`S.needStarter`) and pick one of `K.STARTERS` (Thalnir tank, Krothar warrior, Zephara assassin, Ithyra mage; all Epic, four essences). Chapter I builds the team: stage 1 is solo vs 1 enemy per phase, stages 2-3 are 2 enemies per phase (`CHAPTERS[0].shapes`), and first clears of stages 1-3 unlock Draelyn, Bromir and Skavren, who join the team while it has fewer than 4. Grythor, Vaessa and Brukkar unlock on stage 3 of Chapters II-IV.
- **Enemies:** 50 in `ENEMIES` (Common/Uncommon/Rare), all capturable via `CAPTURE_ROLE`. Enemies 26-50 have English ids (`gravestalker` … `thundergolem`). Each chapter has a themed `pool` (regular + elite, 8-11 distinct foes per chapter); `stageGroup` mixes them so the phases of a stage differ.
- **Campaign:** `CHAPTERS` (10 × 7 stages, names/desc/set per chapter). Stage 1–6 drop weapon/helmet/shield/gloves/chest/boots, stage 7 = chapter boss, random slot. Each chapter drops its own gear set.
- **Campaign difficulties** (`K.DIFFS`): Easy → Normal → Hard → Brutal → Nightmare, each the whole campaign again in a higher level band: enemy level = `base + stage level × slope` (`K.diffLvl(st, d)`: Normal ~19-64 … Nightmare ~51-84), strength `stageDiff(st, d)` = `f × (1 + chDiff × chapter × spread)`. So a harder difficulty is a challenge from its first chapter, not trivial early and a wall late. A difficulty opens once all 70 stages of the previous one are cleared. Gear drops only the difficulty's `rars` (Easy Uncommon/Rare, Normal Rare/Epic, Hard Epic/Legendary, Brutal Legendary, Nightmare Legendary/Mythical); `stageLoot(st, d)` gives the chance of the higher rarity (grows per chapter and on boss stages). Hero unlocks only happen on Easy. Save: `S.cleared` = Easy progress, `S.dcl[d]` = progress on difficulty d ≥ 1, `S.diff` = selected (the old Brutal mode `S.clearedHard` became Normal progress in `fixup()`).
- **Gear:** rarities 0-5; index 5 **Mythical** (red, `--r5`) is gear only (Nightmare), 25% stronger substats (`subScale`). Loot tables: `genGear({ rars, up })`; without `rars` (starting gear) rarity rolls freely. Boss Hall loot: `bossLoot(n)` (Rare/Epic on levels 1-4, Epic/Legendary on 5-8, Legendary on 9-10). **Sets do not stack**: `activeSets` counts each set once. Crit rate is capped at `CRIT_CAP` (75%), also with buffs.
- **Phases:** every stage and Boss Hall level is `PHASES` (3) fights in a row. `STAGES[i].phases` holds 3 equal-size groups (`foes` = the last one; the chapter boss is in phase 3); `bossPhases(id, n)` = two groups of 3 minions sharing the boss's essence, then the boss. Between phases `phaseRest(heroes)`: survivors +15% HP, cooldowns reset, effects cleared, the fallen stay down. Rewards (`winXp`, `winSilver`, player XP) are per cleared stage and were doubled for this. The app walks survivors off to the right between phases (`nextPhase` in app.js).
- **Boss Hall:** 25 bosses × 10 levels. Every level plays like a campaign stage (`bossRoom(i, n)`): boss i belongs to chapter floor(i × 10 / 25); every two levels are one campaign difficulty (`bossDiff(n)`: 1-2 Easy, 3-4 Normal, 5-6 Hard, 7-8 Brutal, 9-10 Nightmare) with that difficulty's loot (`bossLoot`: Epic only from level 3, Legendary from level 5), the first level of a pair like the chapter's third stage, the second like its boss stage; enemies get that stage's level (`bossLvl`) and strength × `BOSS_ROOM` (1.1) via `bossUnits(id, n, p)`. So the Boss Hall is never a shortcut to better gear. The screen shows each level's difficulty, enemy level and drops. **Enrage** (Boss Hall only, `u.hall`; campaign bosses are left as they were): a Boss Hall boss counts its own turns; after `K.ENRAGE.at` (14; `blightAt` 22 for bosses with a Blight Aura, whose fights are meant to be long) it is enraged (+25% Attack) and gets another stack every 2 turns (`Battle.rage`, effect `enrage` with v = stacks), so a team that cannot kill it in time is worn down. The battle shows "⏳n" (turns left) and then "RAGE×n" on the boss, plus an Enraged banner (`enrage` hook). **Blight Aura** (`K.BLIGHT`, Boss Hall only): bosses from level 3 on have 2.2× HP and 0.5× Attack, and each of their turns (unless stunned, frozen or broken) costs every hero 11% of max HP on levels 3-4 (Normal) up to 14% on 9-10 (Nightmare), ignoring Defense (`Battle.aura`, effect `blight`). This makes a healer necessary: measured, a team without healing needs ~1.6× the power at those bosses, while with a healer (even the free Draelyn) the level is about as hard as without the aura. A level with the aura takes ~3 min at 1× (boss fight ~1.5 min; the boss acts ~12 times, max ~16, so enrage at 22 is only a backstop).
- **Fate Altar:** `FATE_SHARDS` (fate, greater, ancient, mythic, legendary) with drop chance per win and rarity table. A Fate Shard costs `SHARD_PRICE` (2500) Sigils. Pity: after `PITY_EPIC` (40) summons without an Epic or better, the next one is Epic (`S.pity`); only `PITY_SHARDS` (Ancient, Mythic, Legendary) count and trigger it.
- **Difficulty / grind:** `K.TUNE` in engine.js: `chDiff` (0.15 per chapter from Chapter III on), `chBoss` (0.7, the chapter boss unit itself), `bossWall` (1, whole boss stage), `wall` (1.4, see walls), `xp`/`silver` scale rewards. **Attrition** (`TUNE.hit` 0.75, `TUNE.foeHp` 1.5 from `TUNE.hitFrom` = Chapter III, regular campaign enemies only, chapter bosses untouched): softer hits but more HP, so fights are a steady stream of damage that healing absorbs; a team without healing needs ~1.4× the power (harder hits did the opposite: burst kills heroes before a healer helps). A middle way on purpose: in the campaign a healer clearly helps, in the Boss Hall (Blight Aura) it is required. **Walls:** two stages per chapter from Chapter II on (`K.WALLS`, never the boss stage; `isWall(st)`) are `TUNE.wall` stronger, so the player has to farm earlier stages first. They are deliberately not marked: players find out by losing and have to build their team up. `STAGE_PW` (engine.js) is a per-stage correction measured with `balance-sim.html?calib=1`: it removed spikes (4-foe elite stages, chapter bosses) so difficulty climbs ~5% per stage; re-measure it when enemies, chapter pools or stage shapes change. Walls are intended: late stages need farmed gear (upgrades cost Sigils) and summoned Epic/Legendary heroes. Measured: Easy ~1,300-1,900 battles with walls and enrage (`campaign-sim.cjs` must PASS 4×; its player brings a healer from Chapter IV on); all five difficulties ~5,000-16,000 battles for most players, unlucky rosters stay stuck on Nightmare until they summon better heroes (`balance-sim.html`, run it from a local web server next to engine.js).
- **Player profile** (`S.p`, app.js): name (first change free, then `RENAME_COST` = 2500 silver; `S.p.renames` counts changes), avatar (a hero id; without one the starter hero `S.starter` is shown, see `avatarId`), player level + XP, stats. Player XP comes from battles (`playerWinXp`, curve `pxNeed`); level-ups pay silver, every 5th level a Greater Fate Shard. `pxNeed(l) = 60l + 12l²`: level 10 (Boss Hall) takes ~60-75 battles, around Easy Chapter IV. `UNLOCKS` gates buildings: Fate Altar at player level 5 (`PLAYER_UNLOCK`, also in level-up messages), Arena and Guilds after clearing Chapter II and Boss Hall after Chapter III on Easy (`isOpen(s, t)`, `needTxt`/`needTag` for the texts and badges). The header `#account` button opens the profile screen (tab `profiel`), which also holds the cloud account section (`FFH_CLOUD.info()` / `openAccount()`).
- **Campaign screen:** under the chapter title a set banner (`setBanner`) shows the set the chapter drops, its bonus, the rarities on this difficulty and how many pieces the player owns. A cleared stage tile has two buttons: Replay and **Auto ×10** (open once the whole chapter is cleared on that difficulty): `startCampaign(i, rep)` with `rep = { n, k, won }` plays the stage on auto, and after each win `repStep` starts the next one after a 3 s countdown (Stop keeps the result open; it waits while an unlock message is open); a defeat or the tenth battle ends the run.
- **Music** (`MUSIC` in app.js): 8-bit background music made live with WebAudio (pulse lead, square arpeggio, triangle bass, noise drums), written as notes per eighth note in `TRACKS`; a Web Worker tick schedules ahead, so it keeps time in a hidden tab. `home` plays on every menu screen, battles are silent for now (battle and boss tracks still to come). `S.music` (default on, button in the Town Hall settings) is separate from `S.sound`; it starts after the first click (browser rule).
- **Status badges** (app.js `fxBadge(k, n)`): buffs and debuffs on units are square icon badges (`FXP` paths via `FX_ICON`/`fxSvg`): green for buffs, red for debuffs, their own colours for Burn/Poison/Bleed/Freeze/Stun/Shield, ▲/▼ for stat ups and downs, turns left in a corner tag. Enrage pulses; the boss enrage clock is `.fxi.clock` (red at 3 turns or less). The Guide's effect list uses the same badges.
- **Auto battle** (`S.auto`) opens after the first clear of Chapter I · Stage 1 (`autoOk()`, with an unlock popup) and is remembered: once switched on it stays on for every battle until turned off. **Focus:** on auto (not in the arena) tapping an enemy sets `Battle.focus`; heroes aim single-target skills at it (`focusTarget`, taunt still wins), shown by a red target marker. **First battle coach:** in Chapter I · Stage 1 the first time (`TUT`), `coach()` explains goal, phases and turn order (card over the battlefield), gives tips on the first player turns and after phase 1, and the victory modal lists what to do next. Guide, auto, speed and give up are small round icon buttons (`.b-ctl .rb`) over the top right of the battle view. The **Guide** button (`#b-help`) opens the guide in a popup (`#guide-pop`) and pauses the battle: `setPaused` stops the battle clock (`clock()`), and every battle wait (`later`, `sleep`, `tween`, `addFx`, projectiles) holds until it resumes. **Background play:** when the tab is hidden those waits run on a Web Worker timer (`bgSleep`, not throttled like page timers) and tweens step on it too (`kick()` on `visibilitychange`), so a battle plays on at its normal pace instead of freezing; only the drawing stops. **Damage meter:** `Battle.meter` (per unit: damage and healing by source, skill or effect name, so Burn/Poison/Bleed ticks count for whoever applied them; damage taken), added up over the phases (`addMeter`) and shown collapsed in the result modal (`meterHtml`).
- **Battle speed** (app.js `SPEED_UNLOCK`): 2× from Chapter I · Stage 4, 3× from Chapter III · Stage 1, 5× from Chapter II · Stage 1 but only when replaying a cleared stage or beaten Boss Hall level. `S.speed` is the preferred speed; `spd` is what the current battle runs at. At 5× (`calm()`) there are no screen flashes or shakes, hit flashes are faint and combat sounds are throttled (`SFX.calm`).
- **Feeding and skills:** any hero outside the team, or a spare copy (`S.fodder[id]`: captured enemies and Fate Altar duplicates), can be fed for XP (`feedXp`: a fixed amount by the food's rarity (`FEED_BASE`) and level, +10% per food level; it does not depend on the hero that eats it, so feeding matters less as heroes level up; Rare+ asks "Are you sure?"). A spare copy of the same hero raises its lowest skill by one level instead (`skillUp`). Skills max at `SKILL_MAX` (5), +`SKILL_STEP` (8%) power per level, cooldown −1 at max.
- **Ascension Stones** (`S.stones`) buy stars. **Fodder** (`S.fodder`): 5% chance per stage win to capture a non-boss enemy.
- **Unlock messages:** `newUnlocks()` after a battle shows a popup for speed 3×, speed 5×, the Fate Altar and the Boss Hall, once each (`S.seen`).
- **Formation** (app.js `formation`): tanks (role or role2) and warriors stand in the front line (`HERO_POS` 0 and 2), everyone else in the back line (1 and 3); a line with more than two heroes spills over.
- **Hero Gear tab:** six tiles (one per slot) with an Upgrade button each; tapping a tile opens `gearPop(slot)` with the stats, Upgrade, Swap, Remove. "Upgrade all" (`upgradeAll`) spends Sigils on the worn gear, the most important piece for the hero's role first (`GEAR_PRI`, e.g. tanks HP/DEF), each as far as it goes.
- **Arena** (player level 12, needs a cloud account): asynchronous; you attack the saved defense teams of other players (or bots while there are few). The server decides everything: the browser sends its team snapshot (`snapTeam` → `K.checkTeam` on the server rejects impossible levels/stars/skills/gear rolls) and which of the three offers it attacks; the Edge Function plays the fight with `K.arenaFight(att, def, seed)`, updates the Elo ratings (`K.arenaElo`, defender moves half) and returns the seed. The game then replays it with `K.arenaSetup` (same seed, same snapshots, both sides on auto): all engine randomness goes through `rnd()` (`K.setRng`, seeded mulberry32), so the replay is identical; visual effects keep using `Math.random`. Tokens: `ARENA_TOKENS` (10), one per `ARENA_TOKEN_MIN` (60) minutes. Weekly rewards by rating tier (`K.ARENA_TIERS`) plus an extra for the top 5 of the week (`K.ARENA_RANK_REWARDS`: Mythic shard, Ancient shard, 3 Greater shards, 10,000 and 5,000 Sigils; rank among players who attacked that week), only for players who attacked that week; the game pays them out on claim. Bots: `K.arenaBot(rating, level, seed)`. Arena defenders line up with `formation()` too.
- **Guilds** (Social → Guild tab, app.js `GD`, `guildHtml`): browse/search and join, found one for `GUILD_COST` (5,000 Sigils, taken by the game), the guild card with emblem (tag on a shield, colour from the name) and the Guildmaster's info, tiles for Guild Boss, Guild Chest and the locked Guild Shop, and the member list (level, campaign, boss points, fights in their own frames; Guildmaster can promote, hand over and remove, officers remove members). **Guild boss** (`K.GBOSS`, like a RAID clan boss): it cannot die (1e9 HP, `immortal`; Burn/Poison/Bleed use its real HP `baseHp`), a fight ends when the team falls or after `GBOSS.turns` (30) of its turns (`Battle.capUnit`), it enrages like a Boss Hall boss; its essence and boss change every UTC day (`gbossEss`, `GBOSS.art`): five guild bosses of their own (`GUILD_BOSSES`: Zaroth Ember, Frostborn Sovereign Frost, Seraphim Fallen Umbral, The Blighted Titan Verdant, The Endless Oracle Aether; registered in `BOSSES` but not in `BOSS_ORDER`, stats from a Boss Hall boss, art appended to bosses.js and portraits.js), Storm and Radiant borrow a Boss Hall boss; 3 keys a day; difficulties Easy..Nightmare open like the campaign difficulties, points = damage × `GBOSS.mult` (1, 2.5, 5, 9, 16). The Edge Function (action `gboss`) checks guild, keys, difficulty and team, plays `K.gbossFight` and stores the hit; the game replays it with `K.gbossSetup` (type `gboss` in `runBattle`). **Guild Chest** (`K.GCHEST`, same tiers in SQL): Wooden (any damage), Bronze 50k, Silver 150k, Gold 400k, Royal 1M, Mythic 2.5M points a week.
- **Gear vault** (tab `vault`, the third button next to Heroes and Team; `VT`, `vaultHtml`): all gear with filters (slot, rarity, set, worn/not worn/locked) and sorting (rarity, level, item level, newest, sell value). Tap a piece for its details, lock (`it.lock`, never sold, also skipped by "sell weak gear") or sell it. Sell mode: tap pieces to select (worn and locked ones cannot be), quick-select everything not worn up to a rarity, and sell them together; Epic or better asks first.
- **Player profiles** (app.js `PF`, `openProfile`, `profileBody`): tapping a friend row or a guild member opens their profile in the modal: arena (rating, tier, rank), campaign (hardest difficulty with progress, chapter and stage), Boss Hall (bosses beaten, best 3 levels), the best 5 champions by power, and the gear of the selected champion.
- **Daily login reward** (`LOGIN_REWARDS`, `showLogin`): once per UTC day on the homebase (after the first battle and the tour) a popup gives that day's reward of a 7-day cycle (2,000 Sigils, 2 Greater, 2 Greater, 5,000 Sigils, 10,000 Sigils, 10 Stones, 1 Ancient Fate Shard); missing a day resets nothing. `S.login = { last: UTC day, n: claims }`.
- **Leaderboards** (in the Arena screen): Arena (server ratings), Campaign and Boss Hall (read from cloud saves by the `leaderboard` SQL function; only as honest as the saves, so no rewards hang on them).

## Save format (app.js)

`S` in localStorage key `ffh-save` (old key `kronen-van-as-v1` still read). On start the stored save (before migration) is copied into `ffh-save-backup` (every 12 h, newest 3); the profile screen lists them with a Restore button (`backupsHtml`, `restoreBackup`). Players can also **start over** themselves from the profile at any time (`startOverHtml` / `startOver`): the save becomes `resetSave()` (name kept) after a backup of the current one. Current version `v: 7`; `migrate()` upgrades older saves. When changing the save shape: bump `v`, add a migration step, keep old saves working. Small optional fields added within a version get a default in `fixup()`. Progress reset for everyone: `RESET` in app.js; any save (local or cloud) with a lower `reset` is replaced by `resetSave()` on load (keeps name and settings). Raising `RESET` wipes every player's progress once they open the game, so only do it on purpose. `save()` also queues a cloud upload.

Cloud sync (cloud.js): one row per account in `saves`. Each device stores in localStorage `ffh-cloud-base` which cloud version (`updated_at` string as returned by the server) its save is based on, plus a hash of the save at that moment. Uploads are a `PATCH … &updated_at=eq.<base>`; 0 rows updated means another device saved in between, so `sync()` runs: local unchanged → take the cloud silently; cloud unchanged → upload; both changed → the player picks a save in a dialog. Never upload without a base, and never decide by `savedAt` (it changes on every boot).

## Working rules

- After changing balance or engine: run `node campaign-sim.cjs` (or `npm test`) and keep it passing.
- Test in a browser (build, then open `dist/index.html`) before pushing; check the console for errors, also at 390 px width.
- Never put the Supabase service_role/secret key, Google client secret or database password in code.
- Anti-cheat: the campaign, loot and upgrades run client-side, so saves can be edited. Arena results are decided on the server, but teams come from the save (checked for plausibility only). Anything that pays out between players needs a server-side check (Supabase Edge Function).
